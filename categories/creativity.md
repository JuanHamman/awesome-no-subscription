# Creativity & Design

Photo editing, video production, audio engineering, 3D modeling, illustration, and graphic design tools.

---

### [Affinity Photo 2](https://affinity.serif.com/photo/)
:moneybag: $70 one-time | Windows, macOS, iPad
**Replaces:** Adobe Photoshop ($22.99/mo)
Professional photo editing with RAW processing, HDR merge, panorama stitching, and non-destructive editing. Handles PSD files and offers a workflow that rivals Photoshop for most tasks. No subscription, no cloud requirement. Note: Canva acquired Serif (Affinity's parent company) in 2025. As of early 2026, Affinity products remain available as one-time purchases; verify on the official website for the latest pricing model.

### [Affinity Designer 2](https://affinity.serif.com/designer/)
:moneybag: $70 one-time | Windows, macOS, iPad
**Replaces:** Adobe Illustrator ($22.99/mo)
Vector graphic design tool with pixel-perfect precision. Supports SVG, EPS, PDF export and handles complex illustrations, UI design, and branding work. Seamless switching between vector and raster workspaces.

### [Affinity Publisher 2](https://affinity.serif.com/publisher/)
:moneybag: $70 one-time | Windows, macOS, iPad
**Replaces:** Adobe InDesign ($22.99/mo)
Professional page layout and desktop publishing. Creates books, magazines, marketing materials, and complex documents. Integrates directly with Affinity Photo and Designer within the same application.

### [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)
:free: Free (Studio version $295 one-time) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Adobe Premiere Pro ($22.99/mo)
Industry-standard video editing, color grading, visual effects, and audio post-production in one application. The free version includes nearly all features professional editors need. Used in Hollywood productions.

### [Reaper](https://www.reaper.fm/)
:moneybag: $60 discounted / $225 commercial (perpetual license) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Pro Tools ($34.99/mo), Logic Pro ($199 one-time but macOS only)
Full-featured digital audio workstation for recording, mixing, and mastering. Extremely lightweight, endlessly customizable, and supports VST/AU/JS plugins. The $60 license is for personal and small business use.

### [Pixelmator Pro](https://www.pixelmator.com/pro/)
:moneybag: $50 one-time | macOS
**Replaces:** Adobe Photoshop ($22.99/mo)
macOS-native image editor with ML-powered tools for photo enhancement, graphic design, and illustration. Takes full advantage of Apple Silicon and Metal for excellent performance. Clean, intuitive interface.

### [Capture One](https://www.captureone.com/)
:moneybag: $300 one-time (perpetual license available) | Windows, macOS
**Replaces:** Adobe Lightroom ($10.99/mo)
Professional RAW photo editor and asset manager with industry-leading color science. Preferred by many fashion and commercial photographers. Perpetual license option available alongside subscription.

### [Krita](https://krita.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Adobe Photoshop for digital painting and illustration
Digital painting application built by artists, for artists. Extensive brush engines, stabilizers, animation tools, and a non-destructive layer system. Ideal for concept art, illustration, comics, and textures.

### [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Adobe Illustrator ($22.99/mo)
Professional-quality vector graphics editor for creating logos, diagrams, illustrations, and complex SVG artwork. Supports SVG as its native format with import/export for many other formats.

### [Blender](https://www.blender.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Autodesk Maya ($1,875/yr), 3ds Max ($1,875/yr)
Complete 3D creation suite: modeling, sculpting, animation, simulation, rendering, compositing, motion tracking, and video editing. Used in professional film, game, and architectural visualization.

### [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Streamlabs ($19/mo premium), Ecamm Live ($16/mo)
Industry-standard live streaming and screen recording software. Supports multiple scenes, sources, filters, and transitions. Extensible with plugins and scripts.

### [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Adobe Audition ($22.99/mo)
Multi-track audio editor for recording, editing, and mixing. Supports VST plugins, noise reduction, spectral analysis, and batch processing. Ideal for podcasts, music editing, and audio restoration.

### [GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Adobe Photoshop ($22.99/mo)
Powerful image editor with advanced tools for photo retouching, image composition, and graphic design. Supports layers, masks, filters, and scripting. Extensible with plugins and scripts.

### [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Paid video converters and encoding tools
Video transcoder for converting video between formats. Supports batch encoding, presets for common devices, subtitle handling, and hardware-accelerated encoding. Essential tool for video workflows.
